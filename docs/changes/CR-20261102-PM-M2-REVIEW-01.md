## Problem

The ClientTypeAll endpoint fields are participant counts and share volumes. The previous internal contract named derived share-volume metrics as monetary `*_value` fields, which could misstate the unit and downstream meaning. Instrument resolution also accepted the first search result when no exact ticker match existed. Empty numeric cells were converted to zero, and the requested `days` value was silently ignored although the endpoint supplies a single snapshot.

## Approved contract change

- Rename per-participant share-volume metrics to `individual_avg_buy_volume` and `individual_avg_sell_volume`.
- Represent legal-side share volumes as `legal_buy_volume`, `legal_sell_volume`, and `legal_net_buy_volume`.
- Represent the buy-side share as `legal_buy_volume_share`.
- Reserve explicit `*_value_rial` fields for data sources that actually provide Rial-denominated values; ClientTypeAll leaves these fields null.
- Missing numeric cells, malformed rows, negative values, non-finite values, and duplicate instrument codes are not converted into valid zero-valued evidence.
- Exact symbol resolution must yield one exact instrument code; no first-result fallback is permitted.
- The `days` argument is recorded as requested context, but ClientTypeAll is explicitly documented as a single-snapshot source and does not claim historical coverage.

## Analyzer policy

- Missing evidence produces zero score and zero confidence.
- Partial evidence may score only a signal whose complete required inputs are present.
- Partial-evidence confidence is capped at 0.25.
- No change connects Smart Money to the primary recommendation pipeline. No live trading or order execution is enabled.
